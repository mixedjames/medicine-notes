The Problem

Some (many?) of us still learn by reading.

Unlike 20 years ago, most reading is done on a smartphone.

Yet we still write like we are writing textbooks or journal articles:

  • Long blocks of text in small fonts

  • Few, dense diagrams

  • Static images

This made sense when space on the page was limited, and textbooks were already door-jam-sized.

We can do better

Fortunately, we live in the future!

Reading on an electronic device, particularly, a phone, is a very different experience…

  1. The physical screen is small
    …but…

  2. The available space for content is limitless!
    …and…

  3. A screen is not paper
